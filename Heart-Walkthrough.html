Diagrams are simplified and not to scale. Oxygen-poor blood is drawn blue only as a label (all blood is red). The heart drawing is an original simplified frontal section; the great vessels are straightened, only two pulmonary veins are drawn on most views, and most animations are slowed down so you can watch them. Pressures, volumes and timings are rounded textbook values for a resting adult at about 75 beats per minute.
