        Diagrams are simplified and not to scale. The simulators are teaching models with rounded numbers, not medical calculators: capillary pressure is assumed to fall in a straight line, tissue-fluid pressure starts at zero and rises only as edema builds, and time in the blood pressure simulator is compressed (the reflex really acts in seconds, hormones over minutes to hours, and volume recovery over hours to days).
