    How to play

    Dr. Akash Garg · BIOL 243 Human Anatomy & Physiology · Chapter 26, Fluid, Electrolyte, and Acid-Base Balance (OpenStax A&P 2e) · Learn the steps first in the Fluid and Acid-Base Walkthrough. Calcium, PTH and calcitonin have their own Calcium Balance Simulator.
    Simplified teaching models, not medical calculators or advice. ABG pH values are calculated from HCO₃⁻ and PaCO₂ with the Henderson-Hasselbalch equation and rounded. Simulator time is compressed and its numbers are approximate. In the fluid model, 0.9% NaCl and lactated Ringer's are both treated as exactly isotonic, glucose in D5W is assumed to be used up quickly, and blood loss is modeled as loss of isotonic plasma. Cell size changes are exaggerated so you can see them. Diagrams are simplified and not to scale.
