    The big idea: every vertebra has the same basic plan (a body in front, a vertebral arch behind it, and processes sticking out), but each region has a giveaway feature. Find the giveaway and you know the region. The adult column has 7 cervical, 12 thoracic and 5 lumbar vertebrae, then the sacrum (5 fused) and the coccyx (3 to 5 fused, usually 4).

    The big idea: the axial skeleton is the central axis: skull, hyoid, vertebral column (including the sacrum and coccyx) and the thoracic cage. The appendicular skeleton is everything that hangs off it: the pectoral and pelvic girdles plus the limbs. Click bones, learn names, and chase your best score.
