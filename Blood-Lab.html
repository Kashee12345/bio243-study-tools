How to read the slide

  • Anti-A clumps → A antigen present.
  • Anti-B clumps → B antigen present.
  • Anti-Rh clumps → Rh positive. Smooth → Rh negative.
  • No clumping in anti-A or anti-B → type O.

Your goalFind every donor bag this patient can safely receive. Unsafe bags are explained, not punished.

Antigens vs antibodies. Antigens are markers on the donor's red cells. Antibodies are proteins in the recipient's plasma. A transfusion is unsafe if the recipient has an antibody that matches any antigen on the donor's cells.

This game is about red cell transfusions. The small amount of donor plasma is ignored, as it is in OpenStax's simple rules.
