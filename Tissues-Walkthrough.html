Every picture here is drawn by code, not photographed. The round "microscope" views imitate an H&E stain (pink = eosin, purple = hematoxylin). Diagrams are simplified and not to scale, fewer cells and layers are drawn than real tissue has, and healing and contraction are sped up a lot.
