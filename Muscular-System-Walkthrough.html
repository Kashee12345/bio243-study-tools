Diagrams are simplified and not to scale. Muscles are drawn as single bands between their origin (O) and insertion (I); many real muscles have several heads and broad attachments. Movements are slowed down and exaggerated so you can watch them, and the lever ratios are those of the drawings, not measured values. Elbow flexion follows the textbook example (biceps brachii as prime mover, brachialis as synergist). In the wrist drawing the flexor and extensor origins are shown on the palm and back sides for clarity; they really start on the medial and lateral epicondyles.
