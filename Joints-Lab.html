Joints Lab

How bones connect, how much they move, and the names for every movement. Nothing here is graded.

The big idea: every joint can be sorted two ways. Structure asks what holds the bones together: fibers, cartilage, or a fluid-filled cavity. Function asks how much the joint can move: not at all, a little, or freely. The freely movable joints are synovial joints, and their shape decides which way they can move.

Notice the pattern: the less a joint moves, the more stable it is. The more it moves, the easier it is to injure.
