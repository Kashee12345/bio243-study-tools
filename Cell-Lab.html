Ask yourself

  1. Is it a big particle or a lot of fluid wrapped in membrane? Endo- or exocytosis.
  2. Is it only water? Osmosis.
  3. Is it going uphill (low to high)? Using ATP directly is primary active; riding another ion's gradient is secondary active.
  4. Downhill: through the lipids is simple diffusion; through a channel or carrier is facilitated diffusion.

    Clues to look for

    • Interphase: a whole nucleus, loose chromatin, nucleolus visible.
    • Prophase: X-shaped chromosomes inside a nucleus; spindle starting.
    • Prometaphase: envelope in pieces; fibers grabbing chromosomes.
    • Metaphase: chromosomes in one line across the middle.
    • Anaphase: two sets of V-shaped chromatids moving apart.
    • Telophase: two new nuclei at the ends; cell starts to pinch.
    • Cytokinesis: deep furrow splitting the cytoplasm.
